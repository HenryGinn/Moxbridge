\tocsection{Box From a Rectangle}

Consider a rectangle with side lengths $a$ and $b$. Squares with a side length of $x$ are cut out of each corner as shown in figure~\ref{fig:BoxFromARectangle_Rectangle}. A box without a top is then formed by folding the sides up, as shown in figure~\ref{fig:BoxFromARectangle_BoxAfterFolding}. What value of $x$ maximises the volume of the box for a given $a$ and $b$?

\begin{hints}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Form an equation for the volume, differentiate, set equal to 0 and solve.
	\item You also need to prove that the value you get is a minimum.
\end{enumerate}

\end{hints}

\begin{solution}

The height of the box is $x$ and the side lengths are $a - 2x$ and $b - 2x$. This gives the volume of the box as $f(x) = x(a - 2x)(b - 2x) = 4x^3 - 2x^2(a + b) + abx$. Differentiating gives $f'(x) = 12x^2 - 4x(a + b) + ab$ which has roots at $x_\pm = \frac{1}{6} \left( a + b \pm \sqrt{a^2 - ab + b^2} \right)$.

We present two ways to show that this is a minimum. The first method uses the second derivative as given in equation~\ref{eqn:BoxFromARectangle_SecondDerivative}. This shows that $x_-$ is a maximum as desired as $f''(x_0) < 0$ and $x_+$ can be rejected.
\begin{equation}
	f''(x_\pm) = 24x_\pm - 4(a + b) = 4\left( a + b \pm \sqrt{a^2 - ab + b^2} - (a + b) \right) = \pm \sqrt{a^2 - ab + b^2}
	\label{eqn:BoxFromARectangle_SecondDerivative}
\end{equation}

The second method uses the fact that geometrically we only expect a single stationary point. At $x = 0$ and $x = \min{\frac{a}{2}, \frac{b}{2}}$ the volume is 0, therefore this stationary point should be a maximum. It is therefore sufficient to reject one of the solutions we found and we do this by showing that $x_+ > \min{\frac{a}{2}, \frac{b}{2}}$. Without loss of generality we assume $a < b$ and assume for a contradiction that $x_+ < \frac{a}{2}$. This is done in equation~\ref{eqn:BoxFromARectangle_EliminateXPlus}.
\begin{equation}
	\begin{aligned}
		& x_+ < \frac{a}{2}  \\
		\implies & \frac{1}{6} \left( a + b + \sqrt{a^2 - ab + b^2} \right) < \frac{a}{2}  \\
		\implies & a + b + \sqrt{a^2 - ab + b^2} < 3a  \\
		\implies & a^2 - ab + b^2 < (2a - b)^2 = 4a^2 - 4ab + b^2  \\
		\implies & 0 < 3a^2 - 3ab = 3a(a - b) < 0
	\end{aligned}
	\label{eqn:BoxFromARectangle_EliminateXPlus}
\end{equation}

\end{solution}
